# Voltura Earner 1.0.1

Keep your earnings in sight, with more room for your work.

- **Meet Minimal view.** See your net earnings and daily progress in a smaller window that fits neatly beside your work. It adjusts its width to keep the amount and controls readable.
- **Switch views with a click.** Use the buttons in the top-right corner to move between Main, Compact, and Minimal. Switching between Main and Compact hides the previous window while your tracking continues.
- **Start or pause from the status pill.** Click **Paused** to start tracking or **Tracking** to pause, in any view. Hover over it for a helpful reminder.
- **Find the details when you need them.** In Minimal view, hover over your earnings to see the gross amount and any daily cost. Hover over the progress bar to see time worked and any overtime.
- **Keep Earner close at hand.** The main window's pin now sits beside the status pill, matching the smaller views and making it easier to keep Earner above other windows.
