# Voltura Earner 1.0.0

See what your working day earns, as you work.

- Choose a task and start tracking. Follow your time and earnings live, with your daily costs already deducted from the net total.
- Choose how overtime works for you: stop at your daily hours, keep earning at your normal rate, or set a different overtime rate.
- Start overtime early from the + beside Start, or choose regular time and let Earner switch at your daily limit.
- Click the icon beside the Windows clock for a compact live view. Start or pause from there, drag it wherever you like, and pin it to keep it visible above other windows.
- See one work-log row per task and day, with total time, overtime, and earnings for your selected period. Add missing time, review individual entries, or correct a mistake.
- Close the main window to keep Earner running beside the clock. Choose Exit from its tray menu when you are finished.
- Save Excel reports for a day, week, month, year, or your complete history, including time worked, earnings, and overtime.
- Make Earner fit your day with light or dark appearance, your own tasks, and optional startup and report settings.
- Choose your currency from a list of names and codes, or type your own short label. New profiles start with USD.
- Use Earner in 21 languages. Follow your Windows language or choose your own, including translated menus, messages, and Excel reports.
- Keep your work on your computer. Tracking and reports work offline, without an account.
